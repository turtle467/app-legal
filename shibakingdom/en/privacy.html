PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Effective: August 22, 2026

Shiba Kingdom (the “App”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the App handles and why.

1. Game progress

No account registration is required, and the App does not ask for your name, address, or phone number. Game progress—including bones, Golden Bones, collected Shibas, kingdom progress, achievements, daily bonuses, play records, settings, and purchased items—is stored on your device. If iCloud is available, it is synchronized through your own iCloud Key-Value Store. The developer does not view this progress data.

2. Support inquiries

If you use in-app support, the message, category, optional reply address, app and iOS versions, device type, language and region, and submission time are stored in Apple CloudKit. This information is used only to answer inquiries and improve the App. A reply address is optional. To request deletion of an inquiry, contact us with enough information to identify it.

3. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to show optional rewarded ads. Google or its partners may process an advertising identifier (IDFA), IP address, device and operating-system information, language, approximate location, ad interactions, and diagnostic information for ad delivery, fraud prevention, and measurement.

Where required, advertising choices are presented through Google User Messaging Platform. The App may also request iOS tracking permission. You can use the game without granting tracking permission and change it under iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from partner sites or apps.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive payment-card details. StoreKit is used to verify purchase status. The App has no auto-renewable subscriptions.

5. Notifications and sharing

If allowed, the App schedules local notifications for offline rewards or daily bonuses. You may also choose to share game results to services such as X; the selected service’s terms and privacy policy apply.

6. Disclosure and sale

The App does not sell game progress or support messages. Information may be disclosed when required by law. Advertising data is handled as described in Section 3.

7. Children

The App is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Because in-app purchases are available, guardians may restrict purchases using iOS Screen Time.

8. Deletion

Delete progress from Settings → Reset Data in the App. Deleting the App also removes local data. Synchronized data can be managed from iCloud storage settings. Contact us to request deletion of a support inquiry.

9. Contact

Use the support page or email turtle467developer@gmail.com.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated when laws or App features change. Important changes will be posted on this page.
